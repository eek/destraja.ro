# Surface brief: /monitorul-oficial (statistics on Monitorul Oficial, Partea I) and the "Publicat în Monitorul Oficial" box on bill pages

Scope: a whole new Read surface inside the established destrajă.ro world (DESIGN.md unchanged), linked from /presa ("Presă și date"). Audience: citizens arriving from a share on a phone; journalists who want citable numbers. Job: understand at a glance how much the state publishes and who legislates, and trust every figure (source and coverage beside it). Constraints: navy intensities, greys and hatching only (risk and coalition hues stay reserved); static SVG built at build time; regenerated by scripts/straja-mo.mjs, so it must read right with a 9-year hole and after it fills.

Concept round: surface seed 8306fb4600b5407ab4d309f2f7119f4e (read). Seven structures ranked; dealt 4, 7, 6 (4 leads). No attended user in this run (subagent brief), so the dealt lead was locked by inference: 4 "ledger of years", fusing 6 (inline numbers as section voice) and 7 (a tracked bill's journey from vote to gazette) as sections.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Twenty-four years of the gazette as one wall of days, each year a row you can read like a ledger line. Refuses the dashboard of KPI tiles and the infographic of coloured icons.

OWN-WORLD: Paper and navy only: a sequential navy ramp on the paper sheet (periwinkle on slate in dark), unread days as fine 45° hatching in line-strong, serif voice for statements, Public Sans tabular figures, hairline rules, white sheets without shadows.

STORY: The state speaks every working day, about 28 acts a day; the Government speaks far more than Parliament, and half of Parliament's laws approve the Government's ordinances; we can show where every figure comes from and what we have not read yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-line serif headline with the median day in it, a one-line lead, the coverage strip of years (read / partial / not yet), then the wall begins immediately below, on phones as weeks.

FORM: ledger rows (year | strip of days | figures) as the spine; sections below as small multiples and ranked bars in the same row grammar.

FINISH: tsc, tests, production build, screenshots at 390 and 1440 in both themes, model review round, detector run.
